3.5 Verify Microservice JAR Deployment
To ensure microservice JARs are actually deployed to Nexus (not just Docker images):
Check your microservice POM deployment configuration:

xml<!-- In application-catalogue-service/pom.xml and application-instance-access-service/pom.xml -->

<build>
    <plugins>
        <plugin>
            <groupId>org.apache.maven.plugins</groupId>
            <artifactId>maven-deploy-plugin</artifactId>
            <version>3.1.1</version>
            <configuration>
                <!-- Ensure deployment is NOT skipped -->
                <skip>false</skip>
            </configuration>
        </plugin>
    </plugins>
</build>


Verify in Nexus:
After deploying, check Nexus:

URL: https://nexus.electra.lan/#browse/browse:redacted
Path: redacted/group/id/application-catalogue-service/0.0.1-SNAPSHOT/
You should see both:

application-catalogue-service-0.0.1-SNAPSHOT.jar (original)
application-catalogue-service-0.0.1-SNAPSHOT-exec.jar (Spring Boot repackaged)
